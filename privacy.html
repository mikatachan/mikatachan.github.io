Privacy Policy: Axis Newsletter Reader

Last updated: 2026-11-09

What the app accesses

The app uses the Gmail API with the read-only scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly, and only on the owner's own Google account. It reads messages from a fixed list of newsletter senders. It does not read contacts, calendar, Drive or any other Google data.

What it stores

For matching newsletters it stores the message ID, sender, subject, date and text on the owner's own computer, in files only the owner can read. The sign-in token is kept in the computer's system keychain.

Sharing

Data obtained through Google APIs is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train generalized AI or machine learning models. It is not transferred to anyone. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Stored newsletters stay on the owner's computer until the owner deletes them. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
