Axis Newsletter Reader

Axis Newsletter Reader is a personal tool used by its owner only. It signs in to the owner's own Gmail account with read-only access (the gmail.readonly scope) and reads newsletter emails from a short list of senders the owner subscribes to.

It stores the subject, date and text of those newsletters on the owner's own computer, where the owner uses them as research notes. It never sends, deletes or changes email, and it does not access any other Google data.

The tool is not offered to the public and has no other users.
